## How to Sign

### Individual Contributors

1. Read this document in full
2. When submitting your first pull request to the Project, include the following
   statement in your PR description or a commit message:

   ```
   I hereby agree to the terms of the cleat Contributor License Agreement
   (docs/cla.md).
   
   Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
   ```

   By including this statement, you are digitally signing the CLA.

### Corporate Contributors

If you are contributing on behalf of your employer, your employer must sign a
Corporate CLA. Contact the Project Owner at the email address listed in the
README to request a Corporate CLA.
