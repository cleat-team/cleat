# Licensing

## Why Apache 2.0

Cleat is licensed under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE). Apache 2.0 was chosen for the following reasons:

- **Patent protection** — It provides an express grant of patent rights from contributors to users, protecting both parties.
- **Permissive but protective** — It allows free use, modification, and distribution while requiring preservation of copyright notices and a copy of the license.
- **Community standard** — It is the standard choice for infrastructure projects in the Go ecosystem and is compatible with the GPL and other copyleft licenses.
- **Corporate friendly** — Its clear terms around contributions and patent grants make it appealing for both individual contributors and organizations.

## What this means for you

- **You can use Cleat in commercial products**, including closed-source and proprietary software.
- **You can modify Cleat and distribute your changes** — modified files must carry prominent notices indicating they were changed.
- **You keep your copyright** — the license does not transfer ownership of your code.
- **No warranty** — the software is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind (see the [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the full disclaimer).

## Future SDK licensing

The core Cleat engine will remain under Apache 2.0. Optional SDK and client libraries, as well as language-specific bindings, may use the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT) in the future to maximize adoption and simplify dependency management for users of those packages.
