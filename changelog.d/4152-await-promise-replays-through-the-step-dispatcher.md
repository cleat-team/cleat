- **`await_promise` now replays through the step dispatcher, and a replay that meets a record that is not its own is a divergence instead of being consumed or re-run (cleat#4152, W4).** The call moved onto the dispatcher the other durable calls use; the events it records, their order, the checksum chain, what the guest is handed on a healthy replay, and every suspension are unchanged, and a table recorded from the old code (34 rows) passes on the old and the new. Three histories that the old code accepted now fail with a replay divergence naming the record: a recorded settlement of ANOTHER promise, or a settlement the workflow made itself with `resolve_promise` or `reject_promise`, found as the FIRST record at an `await_promise` (it was consumed and handed back as the await's answer; the record that follows an await was always checked); a recorded `await_promise` naming another promise; and a first record that is none of the call's own types (the cleat#4157 class: the call ran its fresh path with the history still unread and recorded at a step that already held an event). A workflow whose code has not changed since its history was recorded does not produce any of the three; one whose code was edited between runs can. An engine with no promise store configured is the one exception to the last: it records nothing for an await, so the next call's record sits at the await's step, and it still answers the no-store error and consumes nothing, as before.
