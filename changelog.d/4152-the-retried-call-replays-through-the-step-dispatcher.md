- **The retried call (`cleat_call_retry`) now replays through the step dispatcher, and a replay whose attempts or closing record are not the call's own is a divergence that consumes nothing (cleat#4152, G3).** The call moved onto the dispatcher the other durable calls use; the events it records, their order, the checksum chain, what the guest is handed on a healthy replay, the resume of a policy a crash interrupted (which keeps the first incarnation's step, and so one idempotency key), the host-retry budget and the refusal it records, the attempt ceiling, and every stop are unchanged, and a table of 44 situations, recorded from the code before the move, reads byte-identically for 37 of them. Seven situations changed. **A divergence used to consume** the failed attempts and then the record after them before reporting, so the call that owned that record lost it, and the step count after the report was up to three steps past where the call started; the dispatcher reads the run first and consumes nothing. **Attempts recorded for another operation** used to be reported as "expected call event, got call_attempt_failed", which names the record and not the mistake; they are now reported as a divergence naming the attempt and the operation it was recorded for. **A session that had already suspended** and then made a retried call whose policy was too long for the host-retry budget used to be refused for the policy **and record a `call_retry_refused` event**; it now answers the stop word and records nothing, which is what the host's refusal after a suspension (cleat#4285) is for. A workflow that replays a history in which a different call's records sit where a retried call's should be will now fail at that step with the clearer message. This is also the second call declared in the durable-call result layout, and it is checked against `ABI.md` (cleat#4297).
