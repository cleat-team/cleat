section: upgrade
---
- **The plain call (`cleat_call`) now replays through the step dispatcher, and a replay whose record is not the call's own is a divergence that consumes nothing (cleat#4152, G3).** The call moved onto the dispatcher the other durable calls use; the events it records, their order, the checksum chain, what the guest is handed on a healthy replay, the write-ahead intent path, the cancellation poll, the refusal for a worker presumed lost, the event cap, the hard-stop path and every stop are unchanged, and a table of 43 situations, recorded from the code before the move, reads byte-identically for 39 of them. Four situations changed, all of them a replay that diverges. **A divergence used to consume** the record it diverged on, so the call that owned that record lost it and the step count after the report was one past where the call started; the dispatcher reads the record and consumes nothing, so the next call at that step reads the same record and diverges again instead of running fresh. **The first line of the message changed**: a record of another type read `expected call event, got <type>.` and now reads `expected call, got <type>.`; a record for another service or operation read `workflow called svc.op but history has svc.other.` and now reads `call asked for something other than the call recorded there: call svc.op, recorded svc.other.`. The `actual request:` and `expected request:` lines under it, and the truncation of a long body, are **byte-for-byte what they were** (cleat#4152's divergence-detail change restored them for the calls that had lost them), and `docs/troubleshooting.md` shows the new first line. A tool that parses the first line of a divergence message, rather than reading the lines under it, needs the new wording. The result word the guest decodes is unchanged in layout (error code 1, call-failure code unknown, length at bits 40-63); only the length it carries follows the message. The Prometheus series and package counters the call emits are unchanged on every situation but one, a call made after a divergence, which no longer reaches the service and so counts a second replay failure and no fresh call.
