- **A call made under a retry policy kept performing attempts after one of its events could not be written, and a reclaim then performed them all again (cleat#4210).** cleat#4160 stopped a run at its next host call once a recorded event's write was refused, but a retried call makes several service attempts inside ONE host call and writes each failed attempt's event before its backoff, and the attempt loop never looked at that stop. So after the first failed attempt's write was refused the loop made the next attempt for real, reported success to the guest, and left none of it in `event_history`; a worker lost afterwards replayed the shorter history and performed the call again. The loop now returns the stop before every attempt after the first. Found by the fault-injection harness (cleat#4169): scenario `retry`, fault `dbwrite` at the call's first and second attempt, which failed on every run; `R#3` passed because no attempt follows the last one. Measured: those two cases fail 3 of 3 on develop and pass with the guard, and kill-then-reclaim for the same scenario still recovers.
