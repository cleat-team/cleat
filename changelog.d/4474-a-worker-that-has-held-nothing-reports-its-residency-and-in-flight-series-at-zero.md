- **`/metrics` on a worker that had held nothing left out the residency and in-flight series, so "nothing held" and "the exporter is broken" looked alike (cleat#4474).** The exporter writes an instrument out only after its first measurement, and a worker with residency on records only the budget at start-up: `cleat_resident_runs`, `cleat_resident_memory_charged_bytes`, `cleat_resident_evictions_total` and `cleat_workflows_active` were absent until something first moved them, and on a workload whose waits are all longer than the residency window the held-run series never appeared. A worker now records a 0 when it starts. With residency on, that is both gauges and every series the four residency counters can emit (`cleat_resident_parks_total`, `_refusals_total`, `_rechecks_total` and `_evictions_total`, by the label values the engine records: a kind that cannot end a given way has no series for it), and `cleat_workflows_active` is reported with `workflow_name=""` until a run adds its own. A worker with residency off still exports no residency series. If an alert or a dashboard uses `absent()` or a series count on one of these, read it again: a series that reads 0 now exists where there was none.
