section: fixed
---
- **A run whose history was compacted down to nothing no longer starts over and repeats every call it had made, and compaction now keeps the drain stamps (cleat#4314, cleat#4310).** (1) With a compaction limit of 1 the whole history is compacted and the tail is empty, and the executor used to apply the compaction state only to a non-empty tail, so the resumed workflow re-executed from the start: measured on the real store with a ten-call run compacted after its fourth call, the service received **ten** calls instead of six. The executor now applies the compaction state whenever there is one (`replayHistoryFor`, shared by both executor paths). A limit of 2 or more never produced an empty tail (measured across limits 1 to 6 and up to three events past each: the empty tails were exactly the limit-1 shapes), so the exposure was a limit of 1, set by `--compaction-threshold` or a definition's `max_history_length`; neither has a minimum, and none is added, because the executor is now right for it. (2) `InDeferPhase` and `InDeferSegment` were not carried through compaction (a record that went in stamped came back unstamped, for every event type), so anything that reads them saw a compacted drain or segment record as one written by the workflow body. They are now captured and restored for every event type, in both directions. **What this does not do: records compacted BEFORE this release have already lost their stamps and stay unknown;** the stamps of events compacted by an earlier build cannot be recovered. **One related defect is not fixed here and is filed: a history containing a `call_attempt_failed` event (any call that retried) cannot be compacted at all (cleat#4316).**
