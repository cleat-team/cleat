- **`signal_workflow`, `durable_send` and `fetch` now replay through the step dispatcher, and a replay that meets a record naming a different target, service or request no longer swallows it (cleat#4152, G1b).** Three host calls moved onto the dispatcher the promise calls and `side_effect` use; their events, order, checksum chain, guest-visible results and suspension are unchanged, and a golden table recorded from the old code (`engine/a_signal_send_and_fetch_single_steps_behave_as_recorded_before_the_dispatcher_test.go`) shows every row but six byte-identical. The six that move: (1) a replayed `signal_workflow` whose record names **another target or signal** used to be accepted as the recorded send and answered success; it is now a replay divergence (the identity is target and signal name; a delivery record, or a send recorded before cleat#4102, has no discriminator and is still accepted unchecked); (2) a replayed `durable_send` whose record names **another service or operation** is likewise now a divergence, where it used to answer success and dispatch nothing; (3) `fetch`, on a divergence, used to CONSUME the record before comparing it, so the record belonging to another call was lost and every later step read the history one place late; it now consumes nothing, for a record of another type and for a differing method, url or body alike, and (4) its divergence text is the shared one (`fetch asked for something other than the fetch recorded there: <why>`), whose why-string names the method and url or the differing body, in place of the old text that quoted both bodies and the recorded response. Fetch headers are still not compared. A workflow that replayed cleanly before and does so now is unaffected; one that was silently replaying a different signal, send or fetch than the history recorded now fails with a divergence naming the difference.
