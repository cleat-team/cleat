section: upgrade
---
- **The heartbeat call (`cleat_call_heartbeat`) now replays through the step dispatcher, and a replay whose heartbeats or closing record are not the call's own is a divergence instead of being accepted or re-run (cleat#4152, G3).** The call moved onto the dispatcher the other durable calls use; the events it records, their order, the checksum chain, what the guest is handed on a healthy replay, the interval refusal that records nothing (cleat#4105) and every stop are unchanged, and a table of 33 situations, recorded from the code before the move, reads byte-identically for 27 of them. Six replay-divergence situations changed, and all are cases where the history does not belong to this call. **Heartbeats recorded for another operation were accepted**, because the old loop consumed heartbeats without comparing their service or operation, and the recorded result answered; a history that ended inside such a run was consumed and the call **resumed, performing a real service call**. Both are now a divergence naming the heartbeat, and no call is made. **A divergence used to consume** the heartbeats and then the record at the end of them before reporting, so the call that owned that record lost it; the dispatcher reads the run first and consumes nothing, so the step count after a divergence is the step the call started at. The divergence message is the dispatcher's shared text, answered in the same layout as before (error code 1, call-error code unknown, length at bits 40-63), which the guest decodes. This is also the first call declared in the durable-call result layout, and it is checked against `ABI.md` (cleat#4297). A workflow that replays a history in which a different call's records sit where a heartbeat call's should be will now fail at that step instead of silently proceeding.
