- **`side_effect`, its lookup and `defer` now replay through the step dispatcher, and a replay that meets a record of a different call no longer loses that record (cleat#4152).** Three host calls moved onto the one dispatcher that the promise calls use; the events they record, their order, the checksum chain, the guest-visible result and the suspension are unchanged, and a golden table recorded from the old code (`engine/a_side_effect_and_defer_single_steps_behave_as_recorded_before_the_dispatcher_test.go`) shows that every row but three is byte-identical. The three that move: (1) `side_effect`, on a divergence, used to CONSUME the record before checking its type, so the record that belonged to another call was lost and every later step read the history one place late; it now consumes nothing, like every other call and like its own lookup always did; (2) the divergence message of `side_effect` and its lookup is the shared one (`replay divergence at step N: expected side_effect, got <type>.` followed by the `cleat vet` hint) instead of `... expected side_effect event, got <type>`; (3) a debugger's `ReplayQuit` at the lookup's record answers `0`, not the stop sentinel (`cleat debug` only). `defer` is unchanged in every situation, including the ones where it is not refused (an ordinary segment, after a refused event write, on shutdown), which the table now records.
