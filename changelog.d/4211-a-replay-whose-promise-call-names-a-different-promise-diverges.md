- **A replay whose `create_promise`, `resolve_promise` or `reject_promise` call names a different promise than the event recorded at that step now fails as a divergence (cleat#4211, cleat#4152).** These replays used to match on the event type alone: a call asking for another promise was handed the recorded call's answer, which was success, and the recorded event was consumed as though it were the new call's. A workflow whose code changed which promise it creates, resolves or rejects at a step that is already in its history is now stopped at that step with a `replay divergence` error naming what was asked and what was recorded, instead of continuing on a history that no longer matched its code. A record without the Service/Op marker (written before cleat#4079, or by `await_promise` for an already-settled promise) is still accepted for a matching promise id. The same check is the dispatcher's rule for every call that moves onto it: each declares what identifies it, or says why it has nothing to compare.
