section: upgrade
---
- **`await_child` and `await_all_children` now replay through the step dispatcher, and a replay that meets a record naming another call is a divergence instead of being answered or re-run over (cleat#4152, W3a).** The two calls moved onto the dispatcher the other durable calls use; the events they record, their order, the checksum chain, what the guest is handed on a healthy replay, the child-await suspension word (`1 << 62`, now written down in ABI.md §2.22, ABI.md §2.23 and ABI.md §2.53) and every suspension are unchanged, and a table recorded from the old code (65 rows) passes on the old and the new. What now fails with a replay divergence naming the record: an `await_child` record, answered or still pending, that names ANOTHER child (it was consumed and handed back as this await's result, or this call's child was checked and its answer recorded over the other call's pending record); an `await_all_children` record, answered or still pending, whose run IDs are not this call's (a pending one was answered over with this call's IDs; an answered one was already a divergence, but one that consumed the record first, and so did a record of another type, which now consumes nothing). A workflow whose code has not changed since its history was recorded does not produce any of these; one whose code was edited between runs can. A record that names no child at all is still read as this call's. **Known and unchanged:** a call a guest makes AFTER a child await that returned the suspension word, when the guest ignored it, is recorded after the pending await and runs again on resume; this move keeps that (cleat#4285 tracks the fix, which comes first as a host-side refusal and then as a divergence).
