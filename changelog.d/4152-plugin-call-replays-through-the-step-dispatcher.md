- **`plugin_call` now replays through the step dispatcher, and a replay whose record is not the call's own is a divergence that consumes nothing (cleat#4152, G3).** The call moved onto the dispatcher the other calls use; the events it records, their order, the checksum chain, what the guest is handed on a healthy replay, the re-invocation of a function whose replay policy allows it (cleat#1318), the secret-only field checks and the redaction of a failed call's recorded input, the call-guard refusal and the answer for a worker with no plugin registry are unchanged, and a table of 44 situations, recorded from the code before the move, reads byte-identically for 34 of them. Ten changed. **A divergence used to consume** the record it diverged on, so the call that owned that record lost it and the step count after the report was one past where the call started; the dispatcher reads the record and consumes nothing, so the next call at that step reads the same record and diverges again instead of running fresh. **The first line of the message changed**: a record of another type read `expected plugin_call event, got <type>.` and now reads `expected plugin_call, got <type>.`; a record for another plugin or function read `workflow called p/f but history has q/g.` and now reads `plugin_call asked for something other than the plugin_call recorded there: plugin p/f, recorded q/g.` The `actual input:`, `expected (cached) input:` and `expected (cached) output:` lines under it, their truncation and the redaction of a secret-only field in the live input are **byte-for-byte what they were**. A tool that parses the first line of a divergence message, rather than the lines under it, needs the new wording. The other five changed rows are the fix recorded in the next entry.
