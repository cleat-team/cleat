There is no dead_lettered delivery state, and that is not an omission. A delivery that exhausts its ten attempts is failed — retrying and failed are the same path with the attempt ceiling either side of it (plugins/notifications/background.go, retryOrFail), and cancelled is a webhook that was deleted while its delivery was in flight.

Three vocabularies sit close together here and this table is in none of them: the dead-letter queue holds workflow runs whose last durable call exhausted its retry policy (GET /api/dead-letters); webhook-ingest gives unprocessed events a dead_letter status of their own; and an outbound delivery simply ends at failed. Keeping them apart is the distinction cleat#2050 corrected in this scenario's playbook — only retry-exhausted runs reach the DLQ at all.

What to look for

Filtering by a tenant other than this backend's is refused: the worker scopes the delivery log by the API key's tenant, so a second tenant is a second key. Try it — the refusal names the reason.
