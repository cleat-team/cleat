One order, five steps, and a declared undo for each of them. Place an order, then make one of the steps fail and watch the completed steps unwind in reverse — the work the saga does that a plain sequence cannot.

The orchestration is the workflow. This page talks to a backend that has no pipeline logic in it at all — it starts runs, reads their published state, and forwards one signal.
